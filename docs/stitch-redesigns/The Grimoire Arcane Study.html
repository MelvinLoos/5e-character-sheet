Mage Armor

Lvl 1

Abjuration

Time: 1 Action
Range: Touch
Comp: V, S, M
Dur: 8 Hours

You touch a willing creature who isn't wearing armor, and a protective magical force surrounds it until the spell ends. The target's base AC becomes 13 + its Dexterity modifier. The spell ends if the target dons armor or if you dismiss it as an action.

Identify

Lvl 1

Divination (Ritual)

Time: 1 Min
Range: Touch
Comp: V, S, M*
Dur: Instant

You choose one object that you must touch throughout the casting of the spell. If it is a magic item or some other magic-imbued object, you learn its properties and how to use them, whether it requires attunement to use, and how many charges it has, if any.

Scorching Ray

Lvl 2

Evocation

Time: 1 Action
Range: 120 ft
Comp: V, S
Dur: Instant

You create three rays of fire and hurl them at targets within range. You can hurl them at one target or several. Make a ranged spell attack for each ray. On a hit, the target takes 2d6 fire damage.
