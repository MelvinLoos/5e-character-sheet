Arcane Initiate

Prerequisite: INT or WIS 13

Your scholarly pursuits have unlocked nascent magical potential. Learn two cantrips of your choice from the wizard or warlock spell list. You also learn one 1st-level spell, which you can cast once per long rest without expending a spell slot.

Tactical Mind

Prerequisite: None

You analyze battlefields with detached precision. You gain a +5 bonus to Initiative rolls. Furthermore, you cannot be surprised while you are conscious, and other creatures don't gain advantage on attack rolls against you as a result of being hidden.

Resilient (Constitution)

Prerequisite: None

Exposure to the harsh environments of the Isles has hardened you. Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20. You gain proficiency in Constitution saving throws.

Linguist

Prerequisite: INT 13 or higher

You have studied ancient dialects and cyphers. Increase your Intelligence score by 1. You learn three languages of your choice. You can ably create written ciphers; others must succeed on an Intelligence check to decipher them.

Keen Mind

You have a mind that can track time, direction, and detail with uncanny precision.

  • Increase your Intelligence score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
  • You always know which way is north.
  • You always know the number of hours left before the next sunrise or sunset.
  • You can accurately recall anything you have seen or heard within the past month.

Observant

Quick to notice details of your environment.

  • Increase your Intelligence or Wisdom score by 1, to a maximum of 20.
  • If you can see a creature's mouth while it is speaking a language you understand, you can interpret what it's saying by reading its lips.
  • You have a +5 bonus to your passive Wisdom (Perception) and passive Intelligence (Investigation) scores.
